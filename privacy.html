Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how POCHI Auto Post ("the App") handles information. The App is a personal tool operated by the owner of the POCHI channel and is used only with the owner's own accounts.

1. Information we access

  • Basic account information of the owner's own accounts (account ID, display name, avatar) provided by TikTok, Google/YouTube and Meta/Instagram after the owner authorizes the App.
  • Access tokens issued by these platforms.
  • Video files, captions and thumbnails created by the owner for upload.
  • Public performance data of the owner's own videos (views, likes) for the owner's own reports.

2. How we use information

Information is used only to upload, schedule and manage the owner's own videos and to check their performance. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

3. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any information with third parties. Data is sent only to the platform being posted to (TikTok, YouTube or Instagram).

4. Storage and security

Access tokens are stored privately on the owner's computer and are never published. The owner can revoke access at any time from each platform's settings.

5. Data of other users

The App does not collect data from any users other than the owner.

6. Deletion

Stored tokens are deleted when access is revoked or the App is no longer used. Requests can be sent to the contact below.

7. Contact

pochi.whatif@gmail.com

8. YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services to upload and schedule the owner's own videos and to read their statistics. By using the App you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Information obtained through YouTube API Services is handled under this policy and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page. The App does not use cookies or similar technologies on users' devices.
